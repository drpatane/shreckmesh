# Endless Night 

*observed on 6/25/2026*

I can't believe I forgot the letter. 
If anyone could tell
Which hands had held
The weight of heavy paper
Lined with entreaties of bloody love
It would be her 
The Crone

She knew me before. Late nights grabbing a new medium hardcover A5 LEUCHTTERM1917 to eventually add to the pile of notebooks filled to the seams with words. So many words. 

She knew me during. My undeath. My rebirth. 

And, she knows me after. 
It's hard to find care in this world of night
But, she has always had some for me. 

I had to warn her. 
I had to tell her I met The Witch
I had to tell her I was worried
Because my maker, somehow,
Was involved

He is always planning something
And, inevitably, he will summon me.
He always does. 

"Experiments" she called them
A tinge of resentment
A hint that she could do better

But, my maker kept me fed
My maker brought me into the fold 
I don't think he understands yet
How much I love him. 
It's been so long. 
But, what's the measure of thirty years
In the face of endless night?

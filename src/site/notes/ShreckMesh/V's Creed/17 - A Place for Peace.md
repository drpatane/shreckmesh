# A Place for Peace

*observed on 8/19/2026*

A place for peace
And politick
For plans
And for impressing
The impressionable
And cowering
Before the impregnable

The oldest building in Houston
But not the oldest bar
The oldest bar is hidden
Below 
A place in business since the 1690s
The Vice Royalty Club

The Five arrive with a plan
Speak to the Keeper of the peace
Secure sanctuary for the witch
In exchange for testimony
About a coup. 

In front of an iron door stands 
A hairy man in leather with a collar
Around his neck
With a tag that says, "Dog"
Says, "You know the rules"
"Don't fuck it up"

The Rider asks
"What are the rules?"

Dog says
"Don't fight"

Music blares
It's the blood rave theme from Blade
The Statue gets on the dance floor
The Rider performs a dance of her people 
The Shadow walks in 
A picture of confidence
The Poet joins
Weaving through the crowd 
The dancing sea parts for her
And ripples outward 
Swirling to the edges of the room 

The Poet sees the sculptor, leaning in to the ear of a being 
Postured with sternness and annoyance
and an impeccably tailored three piece suit
A person she recognizes as a "principle of the faith" for Houston

The Shadow goes to the bar to get a drink
and the Poet recognizes the bartender serving her 
And, the Poet's eyes widen

The Rider dances in sync with the crowd

The Statue gets bumped into and notices an older, hunched and hideous one talking to a familiar face.

The Poet drifts to the bar:
"What are you doing here?"
They both ask each other
Almost in unison.
"How do you know about this place?"
The Poet asks.
The bartender speaks of the strangeness of this crowd, 
Of their commitment to the theme of creatures of the dark
"You hang out with these people?" the bartender asks.
"I like the attention" said the Poet.
"Do you still write?" the poet asks.
"Not really" the bartender says.
"Still have the same number?" 
"I do."
"We'll catch up. It's good to see you."

The Shadow sips on a dark red drink and recognizes the hideous one as a person of much importance. A writer of the letters.

The Rider asks her, "What is the plan?"
The Shadow says, "We're going to talk to the Keeper."

Rider, Shadow, and Poet approach the back of the large room, 
where a table and seats are raised above on a platform 
with stairs leading up to it.

On the stairs are two more hairy men with dog collar tags
That say, "Good" and "Bad"

The Rider says, "Ohh, we know some of your friends!"
The Shadow pulls her back.

"Good" asks, "Do you have an appointment?"
And blinks slowly.
"I am not a cat!" The Rider exclaims and hisses.

The Statue goes to the hideous one and the familiar face.
They bow low and introduces herself.
"You don't need to show this subservience to me. 
All are welcome here," The hideous one says.

"Shadow? You haven't aged a day," says the familiar face
"I've had work done," The Statue says, handing her a card.
"I am in the industry if you are interested."
"What is your secret? Are you bathing in blood?" She asks jovially.
The hideous one tries not to laugh.
"What have you been up to?" The Statue asks.
"I've been at the zoo. Writing, mostly science and communication."

The hideous one says, "I noticed your group going to talk to the Keeper."
The one in the crop top had a rather rude encounter 
with a member of my clan the other night. I'm afraid that while my clan
is rather transcactional, it was not the finest. I'm concerned that he
was rather capitalistic."

The statue says, "If your colleage would be interested, your clan
member mentioned offers for help. I am far more amenable to favors
than him."

The Keeper leans toward the Shadow.
"Welcome" she says.
"I assume your presence here means that our business
can begin."

"There has been a change of plans." The Shadow says.

"Please approach," says the Keeper.

On top of the platform, the noise diminishes.

The Rider says, "That is good work!"

The Shadow continues, "Our friend is kindly not pierced through
because there is a change in the agreement."

The Keeper says, 'This _has_ been a change.'

The Shadow continues, "The Witch has evidence of a coup and I wished 
for her to testify."

The Keeper, "Evidence of a coup against our ruler? Who is the accused?"

The Shadow says, "Frank Barrowman," of the clan who seeks power. 
It's always one of them.

"There will be trouble." The Keeper says.

"We are here to help." says the Poet.

"The Witch is ready to be transported"
Says the Shadow.

The Keeper says, "She will be protected under my care as the Keeper. 
I don't look the most kindly at our ruler, but would not like more upheaval."

The Statue appears, "I was talking to the hideous one."

The Rider says, "You talked about the letters without us?"

The Keeper interrupts, intrigued, "Letters?"

The Rider quickly says, "Letters from me."

The Keeper says, "I didn't realize your group was well connected."

The Shadow says, "I run in important circles."

"I should keep an eye on you," the Keeper replied.

"I thought you already did," said The Shadow.

"Not so much yet, but a well-connected group would be useful. 
You know haw hard it is to get a hold of our ruler these days,"
said the Keeper. "The ruler has been rather reclusive.
But, if you don't mind, I was able to secure the presence 
of 'the principle of the faith.' Does that satisfy you?"

The Shadow says, "A deal's a deal."

The Keeper smiles. "Let's retire somewhere more private and continue this."

# Protection 

*observed on 9/3/2026*

The Mortician arrives
Dressed in matching silk
Her mentor no where to be seen

Dog says the same warning words: 
"You know the rules
Don't fuck it up"

She hands her bag to Dog
"We don't have a-"
"You'll find it. It looks good on you."
Dog admired himself wearing the bag
As the Mortician enters
Lighting a cigarette 
In view of all those who
Can no longer smoke
At least, not with the same pleasure

She sees the rest of The Five
Speaking to the Keeper of the peace
And walks over to them 

The Keeper sees her approach
And asks
"Who might you be?"
"My apologies. I'm the Mortician."

The Shadow reminds us of the plan 
"We are to meet with 'the principal of the faith?'
The Principle walks up
"Yes. That is the plan." she says.

"A pleasure. The Shadow." 
she says as she introduces herself.

"I know. Apologies," the Principal says. 
"I like to get ahead of my work."

The Shadow, The Statue, and The Mortician 
Know that this principal hears everything 
Said near a shadowy corner

The Rider knows that no one speaks of 
What happens when you cross the Principle.

The Poet has heard that the Principle went to
The New World after she realized how
misguided her betters were.

The Poet, The Statue, and The Rider get a drink.

The Mortician observes a set of eyes
At the shadow of the bar
And looks back at the Principal
Who winks at her
The Mortician points finger guns at her

The Keeper pushes a brick in the wall behind her
And, it opens

A hallway, grey, stretches beyond
Frosted, plexiglass-lined rooms
All in a row

The Five are led to one of them
A small room, with a small desk, and a couch
The noise from the bar silences completely

The Principle speaks
"So, tell me about this coup."
The Shadow responds,
"First, I need to secure my protection."
"For you, or your crew?"
"I'm the one in hot water, but yes."
"Protection, from who?"
"The Sheriff, My Father. 
Hopefully this keeps our existence continuing."

The Principle reflects,
"I'm aware of your father's activities
and have a distaste for them."
"He is an unsavory man,"
The Shadow replies.

"So, protection from the Sheriff, Your Father, and The Witch"
The Principal repeats.
"Should your information be accurate, 
I should be happy to provide protection. 
Though, this will come with a cost."

"What kind of cost?" The Poet asks.

"The wrath of your mentors," The Principal says.

"Mine is implicated too," The Poet says.

The Principal continues
"Should this coup succeed or fail,
The other Ivory Towers in other cities will not abide a revolt.
And, there is a distrust of those who know about a coup
and pretend they didn't know."

The Shadow said,
"We will talk with the guarantee of protection
and the life of my . . . captive. But, I need to confer with my
crew."

The Principal gave the Five the room.
The Rider said, "Bah-bye!"
And the Shadow gave her a pleading look.

The poet observes a look on The Statue's face.
"What worries you, Statue?"
"We will not discuss it here," they say.

The crew speaks of letters and decides 
to hang on to them, but curiosity remains.
The rest of The Five prod at the Statue.

They relent.
"The Hideous One was talking someone important to me. 
And, he knows that person is important to me."

The Rider says, "I don't have anything to lose."

The Statue responds, "You have friends."

"Don't make me cry!" she says.

The Rider and Statue say something to each other 
In their native tongue.

Soon, the Principal returns.

The Shadow speaks,
"One request. Why did my father want The Witch?"

The Principle answers,
"That would be a question for him. I do not know."

The Poet asks,
"What does protection mean, exactly?"

The Principal responds,
"My colleague will allow you to remain intact. She will
not appreciate an imposition on her jurisdiction,
but she will acquiesce. As far as the designs of your
Father, Shadow, he will know you are under my
protection. If he gets in the way of that,
it will be dealt with appropriately.
I have a request in return.
Our kind are not known for going
So close to the chairs of power 
for a lie. 
We do, however, require more
Proof than an abandoned
Young, leaderless sorcerer."

The Rider asks, "What kind of proof?"

The Principal says it plain:
We want Frank. You, as part of our deal 
Will find and bring me
Frank Barrowman alive. 

The Statue asks:
Are The Witch's wishes known? 

The Shadow says:
The Witch wanted to kill him. 

The Principle thinks: 
I will take that into consideration. 
You should first look into his
Connection with Heinrich Scott. 

The Rider asks: 
Do we need to sign something?

The Principal says: 
Your word is your bond. 
Apologies to your clan, Statue,
We don't need such ceremony. 

Now, one last thing. 
What letters?
You two were lying. 

She looks at the Rider and the Shadow. 

The Poet, feeling the eyes of The Statue
Boring into the back of her head, 
Says:
If you would permit it, 
The timing of this hand is sensitive
And we would like to play it
Another time. 

The Principal considers this. 
"Fine. I trust that when you think
The time is right, 
You'll let me know."

"Deal." Says the Poet. 

They leave the room. 
The five each get a drink
The Shadow sits alone
The Statue asks for something exotic
The Rider walks up to The Hideous One
The Mortician befriends a bartender
The Poet catches up with an old friend

The Rider tells the Five of an assignment to
Proverbially burn a bookstore

# Cold Fire
*observed on 9/17/2026*

Creatures of the night 
Get no warmth from firelight 
The cold night 
The brilliant stars 
The warmth of blood 
Is what we want 

No. It's what we crave. 
It is our only escape from the cold 
Yet those with warm blood try to burn us 
They bring to us their fire 
The thing they used to crawl out of the muck 
to defy the old cold gods 

But, our inner fire 
Our desire 
Our right to live in this night 
Is one that will never go out 
You cannot snuff out a fire 
That does not need to breathe

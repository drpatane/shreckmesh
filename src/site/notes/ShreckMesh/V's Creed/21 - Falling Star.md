# Falling Star
*observed on 10/15/2026*

Falling Star 
Rising Sun 
On this night when we should wake 
You sleep 

Who will seek Julian's justice now? 
Childe of arte 
Turned to dust in the street 
By cattle with predilections of wolfhood 

Wake up 
We need your fight 
We need your curled fingers 
To open cages and free hearts 

You told me once 
Of a life long past 
I see it now
I am in the underground 
Where it is buried 

Maybe I will lay here 
In the dusty detritus 
Of your failure and hope 
Or, maybe, 
I will emerge 
Into starlight 
And carry your fight 
Further than you ever could
